Privacy policy

Life in the UK Trainer (the "app") is published by Corbelio Studio ("we"). Last updated: 19 October 2026.

In short

The app does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no tracking and no advertising, and it works without an internet connection.

What stays on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves on your phone: your answers and scores, starred questions, a paused test, your chosen language, text size and colour theme, your daily goal, the items you tick in the test-day checklist, and an optional test date you enter. This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted.

Backup text and error reports

The "Create backup" and "Report a mistake" buttons only create text on your screen or copy it to your clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to paste it into an email or message yourself. If you email us a report, we use your email address only to reply.

Permissions and third parties

The app asks for no device permissions and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. Google Play, through which you install the app, may process data under Google's own privacy policy.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for the Life in the UK test and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone.

Not an official app

This app is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the UK Government or the Home Office. Official information about the test is on GOV.UK. Translations of questions are automatic and may contain mistakes; the English text is the reference.

Changes

If we change how the app handles data (for example by adding advertising), we will update this page before the change reaches you.
